The Transparent control

Mockup, not the app. Each panel is drawn with the app's real stylesheet. The two None chips are gone from the end of the palettes, which are twelve again, and the control sits with the title: under the name on a wide screen, beside it on a phone. Three designs, each shown off, with the first color transparent, and with the second.

A. Three segments of picturesrecommended

The dock's own Original / Style switch at half size. Each segment is a picture of the pair it gives: both colors, first see-through, second see-through. The see-through half is the checkerboard. The pictures are in the text color, not the palette's, so they read with any palette. One tap reaches any state, and all three are always in view.

B. A switch, then which side

An iOS-style switch turns transparency on. Only then does a two-way choice of the side appear. It reads plainly as on or off, at the cost of a second control that comes and goes.

C. Three segments of words

As A, with words in place of pictures: Off, Lines (the first color), Fill (the second). Needs no learning of the pictures, but is wider, and the words do not show the colors.
