# Colorfetti product copy

## Approved description

**Throw your pixels a palette party. Recolor images and animations.**

Use this exact copy in Help, the README, search and social metadata, and app installation metadata. The first sentence connects pixels, palette selection and celebration through alliteration. The second states the app's purpose plainly. “Animations” keeps the brand open to future formats; it does not change the current import or export implementation.

Lead feature descriptions with generating, choosing, importing and editing palettes.
Describe 1-bit as an optional mode for two-color effects after the palette workflow.

Help follows the selected mode. In Palette, the Palette instruction is **Use Auto, pick
a palette or import your own.** The next step is **Nearest: Tune color matching and
limits.** In 1-bit, use **Palette: Pick a color pair or choose your own colors.** and
**Style: Pick an effect and tune its settings.**

## Writing approach

Keep functional interface instructions short and direct. Confetti wordplay belongs in the approved brand line, where the following sentence supplies the meaning. Describe automatic, preset and custom palettes in feature explanations rather than adding them to the short product description.

[NN/g's homepage guidance](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/homepage-design-principles/) recommends making a site's purpose immediately clear. [Apple's product-page guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/) emphasizes a concise, informative opening and a useful first sentence. The approved pairing follows these principles while keeping Colorfetti's playful character.

Privacy can use a separate supporting line: **Images stay on your device.** Technical documentation should describe actual supported formats and conversion behavior.
