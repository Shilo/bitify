# Colorfetti product copy

## Writing approach

A product description should tell people what they can do before asking them to interpret a metaphor. [NN/g's homepage guidance](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/homepage-design-principles/) recommends making the site's purpose immediately clear. [Apple's product-page guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/) emphasizes a concise, informative opening and an especially useful first sentence. These principles apply to Colorfetti's browser experience; this is not an App Store submission.

Use an action, the supported artwork and the main choice: **recolor + images/GIFs + palettes**. Follow with privacy or two-color styles when space permits. Put playful confetti language in a short brand line or alongside a functional sentence. Avoid wording that sounds like a decorative confetti overlay. Keep Auto prominent rather than presenting two-color conversion as the core product.

The practical length targets here are editorial choices, not platform rules: roughly 4–8 words for a brand line and 12–30 words for a short description. Metadata, Help and installation copy should make sense without the logo. Campaign copy below is available for selection; no campaign line has been adopted.

## Playful descriptions

1. **Small pieces. Big color.** Give images and GIFs a new look with automatic, preset, or custom palettes.
2. **A fresh palette for every pixel.** Explore new colors for images, sprites, and animated GIFs.
3. **Give your pixels a fresh mix.** Recolor images and GIFs with palettes you discover, choose, or create.
4. **Let your pixels join the color party.** Try new palettes on images and GIFs, right in your browser.
5. **Toss a new palette into the picture.** Recolor images and GIFs with colors picked from your art or chosen by you.
6. **Your pixels, dressed for the occasion.** Try fresh palettes and two-color looks on images and GIFs.
7. **Make a little color commotion.** Recolor images and GIFs with presets or your own palette.
8. **Let every pixel celebrate.** Give images and GIFs a new palette, entirely in your browser.

## Helpful descriptions

1. **Recolor images and GIFs with automatic, preset, or custom palettes.**
2. **Recolor images, sprites, and animated GIFs in your browser. Choose automatic colors, a preset palette, or your own.**
3. **Add an image or GIF, choose a palette, and save a new look. Everything stays on your device.**
4. **Find colors in your images, explore preset palettes, or create your own. Recolor still images and animated GIFs.**
5. **Try new palettes on images and GIFs, compare with the original, and save the result.**
6. **Recolor images and animated GIFs with full palettes or two-color styles. No uploads required.**
7. **Change the palette across multiple images and GIFs, then download the results together.**
8. **Choose your colors, adjust the look, and export PNGs or animated GIFs at their original size.**

## Recommended pairing

**A fresh palette for every pixel.**

**Recolor images and GIFs with automatic, preset, or custom palettes.**

This pairing connects the colorful pieces of the logo to the app's purpose while staying understandable without the visual. **Small pieces. Big color.** is the strongest alternative when brand personality matters more than immediate explanation; retain the functional sentence alongside it.

Use the first helpful description for current Help and install metadata. Search and social descriptions add **Images stay on your device.** A campaign line can be adopted separately.
