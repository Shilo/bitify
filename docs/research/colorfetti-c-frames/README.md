# Colorfetti C frame variations

Eight original pixel-art concepts combine a C, a picture frame and interior color fragments. All use the requested Colorfetti capitalization. The range runs from minimal single-color frame treatments to layered paper, fragmented frames and playful color spill. These are editable proposals, with trademark clearance still unresolved.

| Variant | Direction | Tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 Fine line | Thin violet outline and restrained interior pieces | Elegant at larger sizes; the one-pixel frame needs a thicker favicon treatment. |
| 02 Quiet C | Continuous teal frame and three pieces | Clearest restrained identity; less explicitly confetti-like. |
| 03 Warm gallery | Thick golden frame with warm and cool interior swatches | Approachable and substantial; can resemble a gallery more than software. |
| 04 Palette frame | Coral, violet and teal frame surrounding palette fragments | Best balanced option: color belongs to the frame as well as the picture. |
| 05 Pixel postcard | Blue C-frame around a tiny landscape, with an escaping color piece | Clearest picture metaphor; more illustrative than the other marks. |
| 06 Layered paper | Coral frame with a solid offset layer and confetti inside | Adds a tactile paper connection; more complex at tiny sizes. |
| 07 Confetti mosaic | Separate pastel frame pieces and bright interior fragments on navy | Strongest expression of the confetti concept in both frame and picture; gaps weaken immediate C recognition. |
| 08 Color spill | Bright stepped frame, bent scraps and escaping pixels on plum | Most playful; more visual activity and a greater party-brand association. |

The first direction to refine is **04 Palette frame**, with **02 Quiet C** as the restrained alternative and **07 Confetti mosaic** as the most confetti-led alternative. **05 Pixel postcard** is worth testing if immediately reading as a picture tool is the priority.

All identifying geometry uses integer-aligned solid pixels on a 16 by 16 logical grid. The supplied SVGs remain editable; PNGs are available at 16, 24, 32, 48 and 256 pixels, with color and monochrome square app exports at 512 pixels. The 24px samples use nearest-neighbor scaling and may have uneven logical pixel widths. Typographic lockups use Segoe UI with an Arial fallback and need final lettering treatment before production.

The comparison sheets include color, monochrome, native-size and wordmark views. Every variant has a distinct occupancy silhouette. Automated checks confirm binary alpha, unchanged monochrome silhouettes and a mark within the centered app-icon safe circle of radius 204.8px on the 512px exports. Visual inspection is a design check, not a user-recognition study. Thin outlines and busy variants need testing in actual browser favicons and installation surfaces.

No live application files or stored preferences were changed. The preceding broader C, burst and canvas exploration remains in the neighboring colorfetti-variations folder.
