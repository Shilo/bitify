# Judge B summary (59 sheets)

Letters are randomized per sheet, so per-letter tallies mean nothing; only per-sheet patterns are reported.

- At least one output is meaningfully worse (severity 2 or higher) on 32 of 59 sheets. One output is major (severity 3) on 6 sheets: J016, J025, J028, J048, J054 and J056.
- 10 sheets are indistinguishable or effectively tied: J019, J024, J037, J040, J043, J046, J047, J050, J051 and J052. The rest differ only in minor ways.

## Recurring patterns

1. **Opaque core displaced by faint spill (synthetic).** In J016 and J048, one output lets the alpha-1 spill color take a palette slot and recolor the opaque core: red becomes magenta, or tan becomes green. The other two outputs match the source.
2. **Hotspot of grayscale glows.** Lens flare, radial light and trail streak sheets (J001, J006, J010, J017, J018, J032, J045, J054, J055, J056, J059) usually split one way: one output keeps a bright white or near-white core while another dims it to a gray disk. In J054 and J056 one output goes further and darkens the core into a dark spot. Keeping the hotspot often costs a tint (peach or mint) and a hard edge.
3. **Translucent color lost or recolored.**
   - Lost or wrong-hued soft color: the burst fringe in J025 and J049, the glow halo in J030, the olive-green halo in J038, the plum shore ring in J042.
   - Shadow discolored: the contact shadow turns reddish in J058.
   - Glass or film tint lost: the bubble film in J035, the blue lens glass in J005.
   - Animation color sequence lost: the yellow and cyan frames turn pink/peach in J009, J012 and J021.
4. **Dull opaque cores repaired.** Sometimes one output fixes a core the others leave dull.
   - J013: one output has a yellow explosion center where the others are tan.
   - J028: two outputs keep the orange body and smile where one is dull tan with a lost smile.
   - J027: two keep a warm brown for the red and orange orbs where one has only magenta and gray.
   - J004: one keeps saturated orange where another is washed tan.
5. **Halo posterization.** In J011 and J029 one output bands a soft halo or core into coarse visible steps while the others stay smooth.
6. **Palette-forced losses shared by all three:**
   - cyan in res64 and pico8 glows (J026, J022)
   - the red fire glow (J015)
   - green forest and teal water on the island (J036, J042)
   - cream sparks (J050)

## Caveats

- These are visual judgments on composited sheets, with 2x or 2.5x crops of key regions.
- The synthetic stress sheets J031 and J044 have no clear "correct" reduction.
- J051 (dark smoke, auto cap 4) posterizes differently in each output with no clear winner.
